Parking

One bay each. That is the whole rule.

The basement bays at Swift House and Soar House are not communal. Each one is allocated to a particular apartment under the terms of its lease, or is held by MHA. This page sets out what that means in practice, and lets you register the vehicle that uses your bay.

The position

Why the board has written about this.

A significant number of vehicles have been parked in bays that do not belong to them, and it has been getting steadily worse rather than better. Residents whose lease allocates them a bay have found themselves unable to use it.

Each bay in the basement is demised to a particular apartment under the terms of that apartment's lease, or is held by MHA. A demised bay is not a first-come space and it is not a shared amenity: the apartment it belongs to has exclusive use of it. Parking in a bay that is not yours is a breach of the lease, however brief the stay, and it is an interference with someone else's property.

The board is taking professional advice on formal parking enforcement for the basement. Nobody on the board wants to introduce it. It would add cost to the service charge and inconvenience every resident who already parks correctly, all because of a minority. Whether it becomes necessary depends on what happens over the coming weeks.

If you let your apartment

You remain responsible under your lease for your tenants and their visitors. Please pass this page on to them, and to your letting agent if you use one, and register the vehicle that will be using your bay. Correspondence about parking will continue to go to you as leaseholder, not to your tenant.

Vehicle register

Register the vehicle that uses your bay.

The board is building a record of which vehicle belongs in which bay. It means a car in the wrong place can be identified quickly, and a resident's own car is never mistaken for a stranger's. One entry per vehicle — if two cars share the bay, submit the form twice.

Confirmation of your bay is sent to the address the board holds for your apartment, not to this one, so a bay number cannot be obtained by guesswork.

What happens to your entry

The form writes one row to a private register held by the board. Nothing appears on this website.

Step 1
Your entry is matched to the bay recorded for your apartment.
Step 2
A confirmation email is sent to the address the board holds for that apartment, setting out the bay number and the vehicle you have registered.
Step 3
If the bay shown is wrong, you reply and the board checks it against the lease plan before changing anything.
Step 4
If no email address is held for your apartment, the board verifies your entry by hand rather than disclosing a bay number.

Registering is not a permit — yet

No permit scheme is in force at Welland Quarter today, and this form does not create one. It builds the record that any scheme would need, and it lets the board see the scale of the problem accurately rather than by anecdote.

If the board does introduce permits, it will be put to leaseholders first, with the cost and the rules set out in writing.

Questions

The things residents ask.

Someone is in my bay. What do I do?

Report it to Letters Block Management on 01733 898589 with the date, time, bay number and the vehicle's make, colour and registration. A photograph helps. Please do not confront other residents or leave notes on windscreens — it escalates matters and makes them harder to resolve.

My visitor is only staying an hour.

They park on the street. There is no short-stay allowance in the basement, because every bay down there belongs to somebody.

Can I let a friend use my bay while I am away?

Not without prior written authorisation from the board. A bay sitting empty is not a spare bay, and an unrecognised car in the basement is exactly the problem this page exists to solve.

I am a tenant. Does this apply to me?

Yes. Your landlord is answerable to the management company for how the bay is used, so anything that goes wrong lands on them. Please register your vehicle and keep to the rules.

Is the board going to start issuing fines?

Not today, and not without telling leaseholders first. Formal enforcement is being looked at because the current situation is not sustainable. The board would rather the problem stopped on its own.

My bay is recorded incorrectly.

Register anyway, then reply to the confirmation email. The board will check your lease plan before altering the record. Please do not start using a different bay in the meantime.

Your information

How the register is handled.

Welland Quarter Management Company Ltd is the data controller for the vehicle register. The information you submit — your name, apartment, telephone number, email address and vehicle details — is held so that the board can tell which vehicles belong in the basement, contact you if your vehicle has to be moved, and administer parking at the development. The board relies on its legitimate interests in managing the common parts and on the performance of obligations under the leases.

The register is visible only to the directors and to Letters Block Management as managing agent. It is not published on this website, it is not sold or shared for marketing, and it is not passed to any third party except where the board is obliged to do so by law, or where a specific vehicle is the subject of enforcement action or an insurance or safety incident. Entries are kept while the apartment is registered and removed once the board is told the vehicle or the occupier has changed.

You can ask to see, correct or delete what is held about you by writing to directors@wqmc.org. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
