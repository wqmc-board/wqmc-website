Residents' forum

Ideas for the development, raised properly.

A single route for leaseholders and tenants to put forward improvements, raise something that affects everyone, or ask the board to look at an issue.

Repairs and maintenance go to Letters first — not here.

Anything broken, leaking, blocked, unlit or unsafe is a reactive repair and must go straight to Letters Block Management on 01733 898589 or tracey@letters.co.uk. They hold the contractors and the maintenance budget and can act the same day. Posting it here adds days and does not create a job.

Use the forum for improvements, suggestions and notices to everyone — things that change the development rather than fix it.

Good for the forum

Cycle storage, EV charging, planting and grounds, lighting improvements, bin store layout, parking arrangements, security and access, energy efficiency, communal decoration, events and neighbour notices.

Send to Letters instead

Leaks, heating and hot water, lift faults, door entry and gates, lighting outages, pests, damage, cleaning and grounds failures, service charge queries, statements and arrears.

What happens next

Submissions reach every director at directors@wqmc.org. Anything with a cost is considered against the service charge budget, and items of substance are put on the agenda for the next board meeting or the AGM.

How it works

What happens to a proposal.

Nothing disappears into a mailbox. Every submission follows the same four steps.

  1. Acknowledged

    A director confirms receipt, usually within a week, and tells you which route it is taking — board decision, AGM item, or straight to Letters if it turns out to be a repair.

  2. Costed

    Anything requiring spend is priced. Where it is not covered by the existing budget, the board looks at the reserve fund and at whether it is Section 20 consultation territory.

  3. Put to residents

    Proposals that change how the development looks, costs or works are put to leaseholders before they proceed — by notice, or at the AGM if the sum is material.

  4. Decided and published

    The outcome goes on the notice board with the reasoning, including where the answer is no. Approved works then join the maintenance programme.

Being neighbours

Between ourselves first

Noise, parking and bin-store habits are usually settled faster by a knock on the door than by a formal complaint. Where that has been tried and has not worked, write to the board and it will be dealt with under the lease — in confidence, and without naming anyone publicly.

Letting your flat

If you are a landlord

Please pass your tenants the details for Letters and this notice board, and keep your contact address with the managing agent current. Tenants are welcome to use the forum; formal votes and service charge matters stay with the leaseholder.
