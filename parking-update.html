Parking · already registered

Update your record without starting again.

If your apartment's bay is already on the register, you can change a vehicle, add one, remove one, or tell the board about someone using your bay for a short time. Your bay and details stay as they are; only the change is recorded.

Step 1

Get your update link.

Enter your apartment and the email address you registered with. If they match the register, a private link is emailed to that address. The link opens your record and works for 24 hours. Nothing is shown on this page, so a bay or vehicle cannot be looked up by guesswork.

Why a link rather than a password

Nobody has to remember a login. The link only goes to the email address already on the register for that apartment, which is the same check the board uses when it confirms a bay. If you have changed email address, ask for your record to be updated first.
