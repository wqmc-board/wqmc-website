The compliance year

What is inspected, and how often.

These are the recurring statutory and insurance-driven checks on the common parts. They run to fixed dates rather than being picked up when they fall due.

Recurring inspections — common parts, both blocks
ItemFrequencyCarried out by
Fire risk assessmentReviewed annuallyCompetent assessor, actions tracked by the board
Fire doors & escape routesQuarterly inspectionManaging agent, with contractor sign-off
Emergency lightingMonthly flick test, annual drain-downContractor via managing agent
Electrical installation condition reportEvery five yearsNICEIC contractor, remedials priced to the board
Lightning protectionTested annuallySpecialist contractor
Water hygiene / legionellaRisk assessment reviewed every two years, monitoring throughoutContractor via managing agent

Certificates and assessments for the common parts are available to leaseholders on request to directors@wqmc.org. Anything inside your own flat — your own smoke alarms, appliances, gas and electrics — remains your responsibility under the lease.
