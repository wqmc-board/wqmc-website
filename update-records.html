What happens next

Step 1
Your request goes to the directors. Nothing in the record changes yet.
Step 2
If the request did not come from the leaseholder's own email, the leaseholder is told about it, so a change cannot be made behind their back.
Step 3
A director checks it and approves or declines. They may contact you for proof first.
Step 4
Once approved, the record is updated and you are emailed.
